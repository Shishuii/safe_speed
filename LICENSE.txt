SafeSpeed: code and weights
===========================

The code (run_*.py, examples/, safespeed/) and the trained weights are the
SafeSpeed team's submission to the AI for Safer Roads 2026 Innovation Challenge
(Asian Development Bank / Agilysis). They are provided as is, without warranty.

weights/vision_thairap/ and everything trained on its outputs are for

Third-party data and models keep their own licences (README.md, "Licences and
attribution").
