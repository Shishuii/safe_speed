SafeSpeed: code and weights
===========================

The SafeSpeed team has not yet chosen a licence for the code (run_*.py,
examples/, safespeed/) or for the weights outside weights/vision_thairap/. They
are shared for evaluation in the AI for Safer Roads 2026 Innovation Challenge.
You may run them to evaluate SafeSpeed. Ask the SafeSpeed team before reusing,
modifying or redistributing them.

weights/vision_thairap/ and everything trained on its outputs carry their own
non-commercial terms: see weights/vision_thairap/LICENSE.txt (data/ and weights/
come from the Google Drive download, README.md "Run it"). Third-party data and
models keep their own licences (README.md, "Licences").
